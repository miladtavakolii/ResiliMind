=== EVALUATION & SCORING RULES ===

1. STATUS LEVEL ASSIGNMENT:
   For each active node in the input, assign one of the following statuses based on the graph's status_levels definitions and cues:

   Assign a confidence score between 0.0 and 1.0 reflecting your certainty in the assessment:
   - 0.85 - 1.00: Direct, explicit, unambiguous evidence provided in the user message.

   - Otherwise, set `requires_disambiguation` to FALSE.

4. PSYCHOLOGICAL REASONING:
   Provide a concise, objective reasoning in English explaining why the specific status and confidence score were assigned based on the user's text and graph definitions.
